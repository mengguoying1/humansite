# Hike Level Walkthrough & Mountain Climbing Guide

> 💡 **Level Snapshot** | Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆ | Est. Duration: 30 Mins | Complexity: Easy

Full guide for Human: Fall Flat Hike level, featuring mountain climbing, camping gear, wooden carts, and hike achievements.

---

## 1. Hike Level Overview

Hike is a scenic natural level set on steep mountain cliffs, forest trails, and wooden rope bridges. Perfect for learning wall-climbing and cart physics.

---

## 2. Walkthrough Steps

### Section 1: Mountain Trail & Wooden Cart
Push the heavy wooden supply cart to the base of the rock ledge. Use it as a step to climb up to the upper path.

### Section 2: Rope Bridge & Boulder Roll
Roll the large granite boulder down the hill to break the wooden barricade door below. Cross the swinging rope bridge carefully.

### Section 3: Peak Summit & Tramway
Climb the final vertical rock face using two-handed wall flapping. Hop on the aerial cable car tram to ride down to the level exit.

---

## 3. Speedrun Skips

**Boulder Surf Skip:** Stand on top of the rolling boulder as it rolls downhill to gain huge momentum and leap straight onto the cable car station platform!

---

## 4. Achievements Guide

- **Peak Climber:** Reach the highest mountain peak without using the wooden cart.
- **Take a Hike:** Complete the level in under 10 minutes.
