# Red Rock Level Walkthrough & Magnet Canyons Guide

> 💡 **Level Snapshot** | Difficulty: ★★★★☆ | Est. Duration: 45 Mins | Complexity: High

Complete guide for Human: Fall Flat Red Rock level, featuring giant magnets, desert canyons, sandstone catapults, and achievements.

---

## 1. Level Overview

Red Rock is set in arid southwestern sandstone canyons filled with magnetic rocks, desert machinery, and ancient stone catapults.

---

## 2. Walkthrough Steps

### Section 1: Magnet attraction Pillars
Attach the portable electro-magnet to the steel cable. Turn on the power switch to pull the steel bridge across the canyon gap.

### Section 2: Sandstone Catapult
Load a heavy iron block into the sandstone catapult bucket. Cut the retaining rope to launch the block through the cliff wall breach.

### Section 3: Magnetic Rail Zip-Line
Hold the magnetic handle against the overhead metal rail. Turn on the magnet to zip-line high above the canyon floor straight into the mountain cave exit.

---

## 3. Speedrun Magnet Skips

**Magnet Hover Glitch:** Hold the magnet in front of your chest while facing a metal pillar to levitate upwards directly to Section 3!

---

## 4. Achievements Guide

- **Canyon Flyer:** Zip-line across the canyon for over 200 meters without letting go.
- **Bullseye:** Launch the catapult stone directly into the center breach in Section 2 on your first try.
