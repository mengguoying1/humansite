# City Level Walkthrough & Drones Guide

> 💡 **Level Snapshot** | Difficulty: ★★★★☆ | Est. Duration: 45 Mins | Complexity: High

Full guide for Human: Fall Flat City level, featuring drone flying mechanics, bowling alleys, rooftop catapults, and city achievements.

---

## 1. City Level Overview

City is a massive neon-lit urban level set high among skyscrapers. Players must make use of flying futuristic drones, rooftop zip-lines, bowling ball cannons, and giant crossbows to traverse between tall buildings.

---

## 2. Area-by-Area Walkthrough

### Section 1: Skyscraper Rooftops & Ziplines
Attach your hands to the handle on the blue zipline cable and slide across to the neighboring rooftop. Avoid letting go early.

### Section 2: Drone Remote Controls
Grab the dual-joystick drone remote. Push left stick for altitude and right stick for pitch. Fly the drone into the glass skylight to shatter it.

### Section 3: The Bowling Alley & Catapult Launch
Roll 3 bowling balls onto the pressure plate to lower the bridge gate. Mount the rooftop crossbow catapult, pull the tension ratchet lever, and launch yourself into the penthouse exit exit window.

---

## 3. Speedrun Drone Flying Skips

**Drone Surfing Skip:** Instead of controlling the drone remotely, stand directly on top of the drone frame and grab its central rotor bar with one hand while grabbing the remote with the other. You can manually pilot the drone while standing on it to fly straight to the final penthouse balcony!

---

## 4. Achievements Guide

- **Strike!:** Knock over all 10 pins in the rooftop bowling alley using a single bowling ball throw.
- **Airborne Express:** Ride the flying drone continuously for more than 30 seconds without falling off.
