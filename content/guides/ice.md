# ❄️ Level 13: Ice

> **Objective**: Scale frozen glaciers, melt ice blocks using sunlight magnifying lenses, navigate snowy slides, ride mechanical ski lifts, and slide down to the snowy exit.
> **Level Matrix**: Difficulty ⭐⭐⭐☆☆ | Est. Duration 25 Mins
> **Mechanics**: Frictionless ice surfaces, ice melting, magnifying lens puzzles, ski lifts, and snowball rolling.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Level Walkthrough

### Stage 1: The Zero-Friction Slope
*   **Goal**: Cross the slippery slope to the first cabin.
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. Walk forward onto the snow. Snow has normal friction, but **blue ice is completely frictionless**.
    2. Slide down the slope. Keep your balance by pointing your camera forward.
    3. Grab the wooden plank at the bottom, drag it over the ice chasm, and walk across to the cabin door.

### Stage 2: The Magnifying Sunlight Lens ⭐ Key Puzzle
This puzzle teaches players how to melt ice blocks using thermal focusing.

<div class="game-image-container">
  <img src="/img/cover.jpg" alt="Sunlight Magnifying Lens Puzzle">
  <div class="img-caption">📸 Magnifying Glass: Focus the sunlight beam to melt the block of ice blocking the door.</div>
</div>

*   **Standard Walkthrough**:
    1. Enter the courtyard to find a giant magnifying glass mounted on a pivot.
    2. Look up and grab the handles of the lens frame.
    3. Rotate the lens until the focused beam of light hits the frozen ice block near the cave entrance.
    4. Hold the beam steady for 15 seconds. The ice block will melt, revealing a frozen key inside. Drag the key into the shadow to cool it, then use it to unlock the cave door.

### Stage 3: The Ski Lift Ride
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. Outside the cave, you will find a mechanical ski lift system with rotating chairs.
    2. Wait for a chair to swing around the pulley.
    3. Run, jump, and **grab the bottom bar of the ski chair** with both hands.
    4. Ride the lift up the mountain. Release your grip when you are directly above the high wooden platform.

### Stage 4: Rolling the Giant Snowball
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. You need a step to reach a high stone ledge, but there are no boxes nearby.
    2. Roll the small snowball found in the snowdrift. As you roll it over the snow, it will grow larger and heavier.
    3. Once it is large enough, push it against the high ledge.
    4. Climb onto the snowball, jump, and pull yourself up onto the ledge.

### Stage 5: The Frozen Seesaw Zipline
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. Walk onto the wooden seesaw board extending over the cliff.
    2. Walk to the far end to tilt it down, then run back and jump to grab the rope pulley.
    3. Slide down the zipline toward the final frozen glacier.
    4. Jump off into the snow and slide down the final spiral ice slide directly into the exit portal.

## 💡 Pro Tips & Glitch Hacks

<div class="unlock-tip-box">
  <strong>【Ice Slope Speedrun Slide】</strong><br>
  On the first long slope, you do not need the wooden plank. Instead, run and jump to slide down the ice slope sideways. Just before the edge, jump and perform a wobbly dive. The momentum from the frictionless ice will launch Bob all the way to the second cabin platform, skipping the plank puzzle entirely!

## 🏆 Hidden Easter Eggs
1.  **⛄ The Snowman Head**: Find the headless snowman near the ski lift. Look around for a small snowball, pick it up, and place it on top of the snowman. This will unlock the "Some Assembly Required" secret achievement.
2.  **🛷 Sledding Champ**: Find the wooden sled in the mountain cabin. Drag it to the highest peak, sit on it (by grabbing the handles), and slide down all the way to the lake without falling off.
