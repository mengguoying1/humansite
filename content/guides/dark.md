# 🌃 Level 11: Dark

> **Objective**: Navigate the spooky nighttime estate, restore power to the security gates using wire circuits, climb the rotating clock tower gears, and fire up the steam generator to ride the pulley zipline to the exit.
> **Level Matrix**: Difficulty ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ | Est. Duration 30 Mins
> **Mechanics**: Wiring circuits, heavy axe swinging, gear platforming, coal shoveling, and high-tension wire sliding.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Level Walkthrough

### Stage 1: The Haunted Starting Ledge
*   **Goal**: Reach the main castle courtyard.
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. You wake up on a dark cliff terrace. Pick up the wooden plank lying nearby and drag it across the gap to create a bridge.
    2. Walk across carefully. Look up and grab the lanterns if you fall, or swing across using the iron ring on the wall.
    3. Use the hand lever next to the metal gate to lift it, sliding underneath to enter the estate grounds.

### Stage 2: The Double Battery Gate
*   **Goal**: Find two electrical batteries to power the liftgate.
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. Locate the circuit board next to the locked gate. It requires two batteries (one blue, one red).
    2. **Battery 1 (Red)**: Climb the stone steps on the left. You will see the red battery on a high stone pillar. Grab a nearby bench or box, stand on it, and pull the battery down.
    3. **Battery 2 (Blue)**: Head to the shed on the right. The window is boarded up. Grab the heavy iron axe from the wall and swing it to break the wooden planks blocking the window. Climb in, grab the blue battery, and throw it out the window.
    4. Drag both batteries to the circuit slots next to the gate. Plug them in, pull the lever, and proceed to the courtyard.

### Stage 3: The Tree Bridge & Axe Chopping
*   **Walkthrough**:
    1. You will face a deep trench with a tall pine tree on the opposite side.
    2. Grab the axe and hit the base of the tree (or pull the wooden wedges supporting the tree trunk).
    3. The tree will fall over the gap, creating a narrow log bridge. Walk across it (using <span class="kbd-key">Shift</span> to lower your center of gravity for balance).

### Stage 4: The Clock Tower Gears ⭐ Key Puzzle
This platforming section requires timing and coordination.

<div class="game-image-container">
  <img src="/img/cover.jpg" alt="Clock Tower Rotating Gears">
  <div class="img-caption">📸 Clock Gears: Climb the rotating wooden cogs to reach the face of the clock.</div>
</div>

*   **Standard Walkthrough**:
    1. Enter the tower base. Climb the stone steps to reach the first rotating gear.
    2. Jump onto the flat side of the wooden cogs. Let the gear rotate you to the other side, then leap to the next counter-rotating cog.
    3. Grab the heavy iron key hanging on the wall and use it to unlock the metal padlock on the clock mechanism.
    4. Move the massive clock hands outside by grabbing and pushing them from the window ledge. Align the hands to 12:00 to trigger the drawbridge to lower.

### Stage 5: The Boiler and Zipline Escape
The final puzzle requires you to power up a steam-powered elevator and ride a cable.
*   **Standard Walkthrough**:
    1. Enter the generator room. Shovel pieces of coal from the pile into the furnace.
    2. Close the furnace door and pull the lever to start the steam turbine. The pressure gauge will rise.
    3. Ride the rising steam elevator platform to the highest balcony.
    4. Grab the copper wiring harness from the floor and connect it to the electrical transformer box to power the cable pulley.
    5. Grab the handle of the pulley, jump off the edge, and zipline across the sky to the final exit portal.

## 💡 Pro Tips & Glitch Hacks

<div class="unlock-tip-box">
  <strong>【Zipline Speedrun Skip】</strong><br>
  You do not need to wire the final transformer box to use the zipline. Instead, grab the backup generator plug (the heavy yellow cord), drag it to the balcony ledge, jump off while holding the plug, and snag the pulley wire on your way down. The physics engine will sling you straight to the portal!
</div>

## 🏆 Hidden Easter Eggs
1.  **🔔 Bells of Doom**: In the clock tower room, there are three bells. Grab a stick or use your hands to strike all three bells in sequence (Small, Medium, Large) to hear a secret chord and unlock a hidden achievement.
2.  **🕯️ Torch Lighter**: Find the secret torch in the cave passage. Use the embers in the furnace to light the torch and carry it to the cave altar to illuminate a hidden drawing of Bob.
