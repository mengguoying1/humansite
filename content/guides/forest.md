# Forest Level Walkthrough & Heavy Machinery Guide

> 💡 **Level Snapshot** | Difficulty: ★★★☆☆ | Est. Duration: 40 Mins | Complexity: Medium

Full guide for Human: Fall Flat Forest level, featuring excavator driving, log flume rides, campfires, and achievements.

---

## 1. Forest Level Overview

Forest is set in a dense pine woodland featuring heavy construction machinery like excavators and logging trucks, alongside river rafts and rope bridges.

---

## 2. Walkthrough Steps

### Section 1: Excavator Clearing
Jump into the excavator cab. Operate the bucket boom lever to smash through the wooden barricade blocking the dirt road.

### Section 2: Log Flume Ride
Roll three giant pine logs into the fast-flowing river. Jump onto the logs and steer them down the rapids to pass the rocky canyon.

### Section 3: The Mountain Campfire
Light the campfire torch to burn the wooden bridge supports, dropping the bridge into place over the ravine to reach the exit portal.

---

## 3. Speedrun Skips

**Tree Sling Skip:** Bend a flexible young pine tree backward, hold onto the top branches, and release to catapult over the entire canyon section!

---

## 4. Achievements Guide

- **Lumberjack:** Chop down or knock over 5 dead trees using the excavator bucket.
- **Campfire Stories:** Light all 3 torches around the woodland campsite.
