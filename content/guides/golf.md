# Golf Level Walkthrough & Giant Club Guide

> 💡 **Level Snapshot** | Difficulty: ★★★☆☆ | Est. Duration: 35 Mins | Complexity: Medium

Complete guide for Human: Fall Flat Golf level, featuring giant golf clubs, wind tunnels, carts, and achievements.

---

## 1. Golf Level Overview

Golf transforms the ragdoll world into a giant mini-golf course! Players use oversized drivers, wedges, and wind tunnels to hit giant golf balls into giant holes.

---

## 2. Walkthrough Guide

### Section 1: The Giant Driver
Rotate the giant golf club mechanism like a pendulum. Swing it forward to strike the massive golf ball over the water hazard.

### Section 2: Wind Tunnel Transport
Step into the giant upward wind turbine tube. Adjust the fan speed lever to hover up onto the floating green.

### Section 3: Golf Cart Rally
Drive the electric golf cart down the fairway, hit the ramp at top speed, and leap across to the final hole entry.

---

## 3. Speedrun Skips

**Hole-in-One Rocket:** Jump inside the giant golf ball yourself before swinging the big driver club to launch your player character straight into the final hole!

---

## 4. Achievements Guide

- **Hole in One:** Sink the ball in Section 1 on your very first swing.
- **Cart Racer:** Drive the golf cart through all 3 speed arches in under 20 seconds.
