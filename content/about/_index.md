# About the Hub

This site is an independent, fan-run knowledge base for **Human: Fall Flat**. It exists because the game's physics sandbox rewards experimentation: the same puzzle can be solved with a slow, careful walk or a single well-timed launch. Our goal is to document both the reliable path and the creative shortcuts, with sources and version notes so players can choose their own style.

## What we publish

* **Verified walkthroughs** — step-by-step routes for every official level, tested on the current Steam version and noted when a level was last checked.
* **Physics technique guides** — how movement actually works under the hood (momentum, grab points, ragdoll state) rather than just "press these keys."
* **Co-op strategy** — team carries, lever puzzles, and lobby communication for 2- to 8-player runs.
* **Speedrun notes** — documented skips and their difficulty, not undocumented exploits.
* **Media references** — curated community clips and official trailers, credited to their creators.

## Editorial process

1. **Play-first.** Every route is reproduced in-game before it is written down. We do not copy text from other wikis or video transcripts.
2. **Version-lock.** Guides note the game version they were tested on, because physics updates can change skips.
3. **Source the skip.** If a shortcut was discovered by the community, we name the source (video creator, speedrun runner, or patch note) where possible.
4. **Correct publicly.** Errors are logged on the [corrections page](/corrections/) with the original claim, the fix, and the date.
5. **No unofficial cheats.** We explain how the physics engine behaves; we do not distribute third-party tools, trainers, or modified game files.
