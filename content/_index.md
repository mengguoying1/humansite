《**Human: Fall Flat**》is a physics-based puzzle platformer where every grab, swing, and throw is governed by a real-time simulation. That means two players can clear the same level in completely different ways — one slow and careful, the other launching across the map with a well-timed momentum skip.

This independent fan site collects level walkthroughs, gameplay notes, and multiplayer guides. Game updates may change routes and mechanics; check the game's current version if a step no longer works.

## What you'll find

* **[Level Guides](/guides/)** — Walkthroughs for levels covered by the site.
* **[Gameplay Techniques](/gameplay/)** — Controls and practical movement notes.
* **[Multiplayer Strategies](/multiplayer/)** — Co-op carries, lever puzzles, and 8-player lobby coordination.
* **[Media Center](/media/)** — Curated community highlights and official trailers, credited to their creators.

## Why this guide is different

Use these guides as community references and compare routes with your own game version:

1. **Practical steps.** Guides aim to describe the objects and actions involved in a route.
2. **Check changes.** Game updates may affect routes, so verify steps in your installed version.
3. **Corrections.** If a step is wrong or unclear, contact us using the site's contact page.
